How it works: grab food from a crate, prepare it (chop, boil or fry), then put everything on a plate and serve it at the blue window. Hold food and press grab on a plate stack to start a plate. Holding a plate, you can grab buns, seaweed and tortillas straight from their crate. Pots take up to 3 items and start cooking on a stove; pans fry one item. Take them off before they burn! Throw food with the chop key (it lands on counters, in pots, on plates, or your teammate catches it).
